Yanıtta — AI search visibility

2026
Product · built end to end

A tool that answers a question almost no brand can currently answer: when a customer asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity for a recommendation, do you appear?

  • Generates the buyer-intent questions a real customer would ask, in the brand's own language
  • Queries three engines, detects brand and competitor mentions inside the answers, scores visibility, average position and share of voice
  • Maps which third-party domains the models cite — the actionable part, since AI answers are assembled from other people's pages, not yours
  • Turkish-first brand matching: inflection changes the surface form of a name, and naive matching produces both false positives and misses
Node · Express · SQLite · multi-provider LLM layer · scheduled scans
